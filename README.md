## Overview

[AI Manga Translator](https://aimangatranslation.com/) is an AI-powered comic localization workspace built for readers and creators working with Japanese **Manga**, Korean **Manhwa / Webtoons**, Chinese **Manhua**, **Western Comics**, and **Doujinshi**.

Unlike generic text translators that lose context or cover artwork with unreadable boxes, AI Manga Translator detects speech bubbles, translates dialogue between **English, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese** (with automatic source detection), and renders clean lettering directly inside the panel while keeping original pages available for side-by-side comparison.

---

## Key Features

- **Single Images & Free Guest Trial** — Upload or paste JPG, PNG, or WebP pages (up to 20 MB per image, up to 10 images per batch) and try image translation in your browser before signing in.
- **Full-Chapter Batch Queue (PDF, ZIP, CBZ)** — Import up to 5 documents and 100 pages per batch (up to 50 pages and 100 MB per file). Your browser extracts pages locally; once uploaded to the queue, translation runs in the background so you can leave the page and export completed chapters as **ZIP** or **CBZ**.
- **In-Browser Chrome Extension** — Read raw chapters directly on MangaDex, Naver Webtoon, Kakao, ComicWalker, Pixiv, and X/Twitter with in-place speech bubble replacement, automatic long-strip webtoon slicing, and shared account credits.
- **Manga-Aware Style & Lettering Controls** — Choose from 5 translation styles (*Standard*, *Keep Honorifics*, *Faithful*, *Conversational*, *Formal*) and 3 lettering styles (*Preserve Manga Style*, *Sans Serif*, *Serif*).
- **Side-by-Side Review & Credit Protection** — Compare translated pages against originals with an interactive slider, inspect any unplaced text over complex artwork separately, retry individual pages, and receive automatic credit refunds for confirmed failed pages.

---

## How It Works

1. **Upload or Open in Browser** — Drop individual manga images, import a full `PDF` / `ZIP` / `CBZ` chapter on [aimangatranslation.com](https://aimangatranslation.com/), or activate the [Chrome Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ai-manga-translator/lmjejdalbehpfeceangepppeofoopabl) on any reading site.
2. **Choose Languages & Style** — Select source language (or Auto-Detect), target language (`EN`, `JA`, `KO`, `ZH`), translation tone, and lettering style, then review the transparent credit estimate before confirming.
3. **Compare & Export** — Inspect translated panels side-by-side with the raw artwork and download single images or full `ZIP` / `CBZ` archives from Translation History.

## Architecture & Privacy

- **Modern Full-Stack Web App** — Built with TanStack Start, React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and edge-ready server rendering with multi-locale routing.
- **Local Archive Parsing** — When importing `PDF`, `ZIP`, or `CBZ` chapters, your browser unpacks the archive locally and uploads only the extracted page images after you confirm.
- **Account-Scoped Privacy & Retention** — Submitted jobs and translated pages are accessible only to your account. Single uploads expire after 24 hours; queued chapter sources are kept for at least 7 days and results for 90 days, with manual file removal available anytime in Translation History.
- **Minimal Extension Permissions** — The Chrome extension activates only when you trigger a translation and never tracks your browsing history.
